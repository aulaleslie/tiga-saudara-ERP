## Why

POS transactions completed as Kas Bon can create one or more outstanding Sales documents whose due dates do not reflect the longevity of the payment term selected at checkout. This produces incorrect receivable deadlines, especially when a checkout is split across source owners.

## What Changes

- Preserve the authorized Kas Bon flag and selected payment-term identifier through the staged-payment/session flow into final checkout posting.
- Calculate each generated Kas Bon Sale document's due date from the checkout posting date plus the selected payment term's longevity.
- Apply the same term and calculated due date to every owner-specific Sale generated by split POS posting.
- Add regression coverage for zero and partial down-payment Kas Bon transactions, including split-owner checkout documents.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `pos-debt-checkout`: Ensure every Sales document produced by a Kas Bon checkout retains the selected payment term and receives a due date calculated from its longevity.

## Impact

- Affected POS staged-payment browser flow, checkout finalization handoff, and POS sale-posting adapters.
- Affected receivable due dates and generated `sales.payment_term_id` / `sales.due_date` values; no public API or schema change is expected.
- Affected automated POS debt and split-posting feature tests.
