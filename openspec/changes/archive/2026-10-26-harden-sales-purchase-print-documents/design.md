# Design

## Context

See proposal.md for motivation and specs/sales-purchase-print-documents/spec.md for the behavior contract. Laravel 10 modules use Blade and DomPDF. Purchase printing is an inline route closure; sales printing uses SaleController. Current PDF templates bypass commercial detail breakdowns and canonical settlement accessors. Global detail pages share ordinary detail templates but suppress printing. Sales PDF routes enforce session-business ownership, which must be extended carefully for authorized Global Payments contexts.

Relevant existing contracts: current-product-name-display, purchase-conversion-unit-entry, cross-business-purchase-sale-documents, and standard-sale-document-lines. Preserve their product-name fallbacks, entered-unit discounts, business ownership, and fulfillment semantics. No document references are generated by printing.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:** Share presentation preparation across paired commercial variants, keep endpoints independently authorized, preserve recorded monetary authority, and make archived/global printing deliberate.

**Non-Goals:** Reprice or repair transactions, recalculate historical taxes, add credit mechanisms to purchases, change approval or fulfillment behavior, introduce snapshots or migrations, alter POS receipts, or require full-suite verification.

## Decisions

### 1. Explicit variants and common preparation

Retain existing invoice and purchase PDF route names as defaults for compatibility. Add explicit offer and purchase-order actions, or a validated allowlisted variant parameter behind named routes. Move purchase PDF logic into a controller. Reject unknown variants. Build a presentation payload for each module, sharing paired layouts/partials for commercial rows and totals. Variant controls title, filename, and settlement visibility only; current status is independent.

Alternative: copy full templates per variant. Rejected because tax and balance fixes would drift. Avoid a broad document framework: small module-oriented preparation is sufficient.

### 2. Authorize before preparing data

Resolve documents including archived records, then enforce sales.show or purchases.show and business access. Ordinary printing retains active-business scope. A Global Payments context requires the relevant global-access permission, document-view permission, and access to the owning business. A client context parameter cannot grant access; validate it server-side. Do not mutate session setting. Resolve selected dispatch through the authorized sale relationship and require approved status. Buttons mirror endpoint eligibility.

Alternative: remove session ownership checks globally. Rejected because global printing must not widen ordinary access.

### 3. Current business identity and PKP presentation

Eager-load tenantSetting and use it for all business identity and PKP decisions, including Surat Jalan identity. Remove hardcoded branding and dependence on the active settings() helper in these PDFs. Suppress tax-specific identity and fields for non-PKP copies. A missing owner business fails safely rather than using another business’s identity.

For current PKP copies, read saved row/header tax amounts and recorded rate information. Use saved inclusion mode; never derive historical amounts using a newly changed tax master rate. Where no historical rate snapshot is available, show the recorded amount and omit an unverifiable rate rather than inventing one. Zero-tax historical documents stay zero-tax. Non-PKP presentation keeps gross commercial rows, hiding the tax breakdown without subtracting tax or rewriting records.

Alternative: let historical tax force disclosure. Rejected by the agreed current-is_pkp rule.

### 4. Saved amounts drive reconciliation

Use existing entered-unit accessors and product-name accessors with eager-loaded relations. Include ordinary sale lines and standalone bundle commercial rows once; do not append priced components that double-count bundles. Use effective_sub_total and global_discount_share where present, with a deterministic read-only legacy fallback from saved header discount percentage/amount and row subtotals. Do not assume header discount_amount represents percentage-mode discounts: SaleNormalizer stores percentage mode separately.

Present item subtotals after row discounts, overall discount, shipping, and saved grand total. For PKP presentation disclose recorded DPP and tax without adding tax again to gross subtotals. Keep allocation/rounding at existing monetary precision; show a labeled reconciliation difference for inconsistent legacy records instead of hidden corrections. Preserve manual line totals even where displayed rounded unit-price multiplication differs. Purchase entered quantities retain supported decimals; do not truncate to integers.

Alternative: reuse detail-page DPP arithmetic verbatim or rerun normalizers. Rejected because the former can omit allocated discount semantics and the latter can alter historical economics.

### 5. Canonical settlement with separate credit presentation

Use Sale.getEffectivePaidAmount()/live_due_amount and Purchase equivalents as monetary authority. Prepare active monetary-payment and active credit components separately for sales, matching the canonical joined payment-status filter. Purchases show supported settlement components only; do not invent a purchase credit ledger. Labels distinguish payments and credits. Offer/order payloads omit settlement sections. Printing performs no reconciliation writes.

Alternative: sum loaded payment records or use stored due_amount. Rejected because invalidations and applied credits can make those values inaccurate.

### 6. Approved dispatch selection

Latest-approved shortcut orders approved dispatches by descending ID, preserving the existing latest-ID meaning while filtering approval. Individual history actions specify dispatch ID under the sale. No approved dispatch means no printable delivery note. Preserve existing product grouping and serial deduplication within that selected dispatch; do not combine dispatch histories or expose prices. Archived sales can print approved history through the same authorization rules.

### 7. Focused verification

Add focused feature/rendering tests for the capability, using existing SQLite-compatible Laravel fixtures. Inspect generated PDF samples for both tax modes, each title/settlement variant, and a long multipage case. Test permission denial and business mismatches at endpoints, not only button visibility. Run only new/affected print tests and existing narrowly related tests when needed. No composer full-suite command or repository-wide test gate is planned.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- Current identity/PKP can change a reprint → intentional behavior; retain saved monetary values and test both PKP transitions.
- Legacy rows may lack rate or allocation snapshots → use saved monetary fields and explicit reconciliation differences; never query current rates to fabricate history.
- Global printing can expose another business → require existing global permission plus document-view and owner-business access on every endpoint.
- Fractional units, manual totals, and bundles can drift through presentation math → reuse saved amounts and entered-unit accessors with representative focused fixtures.
- PDF pagination differs from HTML → inspect actual multipage DomPDF output and protect heading/signature layout.

## Migration Plan

No schema migration or data backfill. Deploy controller/presentation changes, routes, templates, and focused tests together. Preserve existing default PDF URLs. Roll back code to restore prior printing; no transaction data needs rollback. Record focused commands and PDF inspection outcomes during implementation.
